# RobustSandwichRV 0.1.0

* Initial pure-R implementation.
* Added classical, HC0, HC1, CR0, and CR1 scalar-confounder evaluation.
* Added protected-first, diagonal-first, and full point-conditioned workflows.
* Added benchmark-bounded calibration with certified lower and upper bounds.
* Added scalar and rank-normalized grouped observed-control benchmarks.
* Added explicit full-regression witness validation and nested-band reuse.
* Added two-direction reduction for arbitrary omitted-confounder blocks.
* Added frozen cross-language numerical fixtures, `sensemakr` comparison, and
  `sandwich` covariance validation.
